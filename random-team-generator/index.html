Balanced team tool

Free Random Team Generator

Split a list of participants into balanced random teams without creating an account. SpinOrder reveals each person’s team on the wheel and keeps the complete result available to copy, download, or share.

Create Random Teams

Useful for quick group assignments

Create teams for sports practices, party and board games, classroom activities, workshops, and other group events. Names stay in your browser while you build the event.

Create teams in three steps

  1. Choose the number of teams. Select from 2 teams up to the smaller of 10 or your participant count.
  2. Add every participant. Type names or import a local TXT or CSV file.
  3. Reveal the assignments. Spin manually or automatically, then review the completed groups by team.

Balanced by participant count

Every participant is assigned exactly once. Destinations rotate from Team 1 through the final team and then repeat, so team sizes differ by no more than one person. When the total does not divide evenly, the first teams in that rotation receive one extra participant.

Random team generator FAQ

Is the team generator free?

Yes. SpinOrder is free to use and does not require a login or account.

How many teams can I create?

You can create 2–10 teams, with no more teams than participants.

Are teams balanced by skill or other criteria?

No. This mode balances the number of participants per team. It does not evaluate skill, age, position, or other attributes.

Can I share the finished teams?

Yes. Completed assignments can be copied, downloaded, printed, emailed through a prepared draft, or shared using supported device features.
