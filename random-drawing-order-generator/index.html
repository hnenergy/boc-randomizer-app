Create an order for an event

Use the tool for drawings, presentation order, turn order, lotteries, and general event sequencing when you need every entered name placed into one randomized order.

Create a drawing order in three steps

  1. Name the event. Add a short label for the drawing or sequence.
  2. Add the names. Enter names manually or import a local TXT or CSV file.
  3. Reveal the order. Spin manually or automatically, then review or share the completed list.

A randomized order, not entrant management

Each entered name appears once in the completed order. SpinOrder does not manage entrants, validate eligibility, administer prizes, support weighted or multiple entries, or determine whether a drawing meets legal requirements. Position 1 is simply the first position in the randomized sequence—not a guaranteed legal winner.

Drawing order generator FAQ

Will every name appear in the order?

Yes. Each valid entered name is selected once, removed from the wheel, and included in the completed order.

Can I reveal position 1 first?

Yes. This preset begins with position 1 and then continues upward through the remaining positions.

Can I save or share the order?

Yes. Completed results can be copied, downloaded, printed, prepared in an email draft, or shared using supported device features.
