Choose students for classroom activities

Use the picker for classroom participation, presentations, reading order, learning activities, and classroom games. Each entered student remains eligible until selected once.

Pick student names in three steps

  1. Name the activity. Add a short classroom event name.
  2. Add student names. Enter names manually or import a local TXT or CSV file.
  3. Start selecting. Spin manually or automatically and follow the completed selection order.

No repeats before reset

After a student is selected, that student is removed from the available wheel. The full student list becomes available again when you reset the picker.

Classroom name picker FAQ

Can a student be selected twice?

Not during the same completed run. Each selected student is removed until the picker is reset.

Can I use automatic spinning?

Yes. Manual and automatic spinning use the same selection process, and automatic spinning can be paused, resumed, or stopped.

Can I save or share the selection order?

Yes. After every student is selected, the results can be copied, downloaded, printed, prepared in an email draft, or shared on supported devices.
