Golf grouping tool

Free Golf Group Randomizer

Randomly assign every golfer to a balanced-size group without creating an account. SpinOrder reveals one golfer at a time and keeps the completed groups ready to copy, download, or share.

Create Golf Groups

Make groups for the day’s format

Use the randomizer for golf outings, league days, casual rounds, foursomes, and tee-time groups. Add the full player list, choose how many groups you need, and reveal the assignments together.

Create golf groups in three steps

  1. Choose the number of groups. Select from 2 groups up to the smaller of 10 or the golfer count.
  2. Add every golfer. Enter names manually or import a local TXT or CSV file.
  3. Reveal the groups. Spin manually or automatically, then review and share the completed assignments.

Balanced by headcount only

Destinations rotate from Group 1 through the final group and then repeat. This keeps group sizes within one golfer of each other. SpinOrder does not balance by handicap, ability, gender, age, or any factor other than participant count.

Golf group randomizer FAQ

Is the golf group randomizer free?

Yes. SpinOrder is free to use and does not require an account or login.

Can it make foursomes?

Yes. Choose a group count that produces foursomes for your player total. If the count does not divide evenly, some groups will contain one additional golfer.

Does it balance groups by handicap?

No. Assignments are random and balanced only by the number of golfers in each group.

Can I share the finished groups?

Yes. You can copy, download, print, prepare an email draft, or use supported device sharing after every golfer is assigned.

Need groups for another activity? The balanced random team tool uses the same private, no-account workflow with general team terminology.
