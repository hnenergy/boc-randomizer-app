Built for common league formats

Use SpinOrder when your league needs a clear order before draft day, including:

  • Redraft leagues setting a new order each season
  • Keeper leagues assigning positions under the league's existing rules
  • Dynasty leagues randomizing an initial startup-draft order
  • Live draft events revealing positions together in person or on a shared screen

How it works

  1. Name the event and choose reveal order. Start with position 1 or reveal the last position first.
  2. Add every team or manager. Enter names manually or import a local text or CSV file.
  3. Run the draft order. Spin manually or use Auto Spin, then copy, download, print, or share the completed order.

A clear way to set the order

SpinOrder selects from the teams still eligible on the wheel. After a team receives a position, it is excluded from later selections. The completed results list shows one numbered position for every team, sorted from first to last.

Fantasy football draft-order FAQ

Is the draft-order randomizer free?

Yes. SpinOrder is free to use and does not require a login or account.

How many teams can I add?

You can enter 2–20 names manually. A validated local TXT or CSV import can contain up to 60 names.

Can we reveal the last draft position first?

Yes. Choose “Last position first” to count down toward position 1, or choose “Position 1 first” to count upward.

Does SpinOrder determine a snake-draft format?

No. SpinOrder assigns the numbered draft positions. Your league can apply its own snake, linear, auction, or other draft rules afterward.
