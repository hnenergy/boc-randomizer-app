About SpinOrder

A simple way to set a fair order

SpinOrder is a free randomizer for creating an order from a list of names. It works without an account or login.

What you can randomize

Use SpinOrder for fantasy draft positions, participant order, drawings, team assignments, classroom activities, golf groups, and other one-time events.

How it works

Choose the event settings, add names manually or import a local text or CSV file, and spin manually or automatically. SpinOrder assigns each participant a unique numbered position and presents the completed order for copying, downloading, printing, or sharing.

No account is required. Start from the homepage whenever you need a new randomizer.
